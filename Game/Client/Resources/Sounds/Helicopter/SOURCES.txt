Created resources
helicopter_crash.wav: 10 seconds. Constant-level flight and mechanical failure leading up to impact. The impact portion is separated so the event can trigger it at the exact collision time.
helicopter_explosion.wav: 4.1 seconds. Mechanical crash impact and debris tail separated from helicopter_crash.wav for independent 3D playback.
helicopter_landing.wav: 10-second constant-level rotor load/RPM transition and touchdown cue.
helicopter_takeoff.wav: 8-second constant-level rotor load/RPM transition.
helicopter_idle_loop.wav: 6-second seamless stationary rotor loop for the landed waiting period.

No travel-distance volume envelope is baked into the resources. Runtime FMOD 3D positioning and attenuation handle distance and direction.
The crash-flight, landing, takeoff, and idle rotor tracks use a 3x mastering gain with a -0.5 dBFS soft peak limiter. The separately triggered explosion track is unchanged.
Output format: 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM, mono WAV. Mono was selected for positional 3D playback.
